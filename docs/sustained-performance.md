# Sustained single-node serving

Historical sustained Qwen3-8B serving controls include repeated C4, fixed-cohort engine-local Restore and the restore-admission stall discovered under pressure. Keep their workload/reuse distribution, exact builds and output limits when interpreting latency. They are not the current official-release larger-model comparison.

The [immutable full report](https://github.com/feichai0017/orbitkv/blob/251f463d8997eff783871ab2820da83a13a97196/docs/sustained-performance.md) retains tables, exact commands,
artifact locations, failed controls and uncertainty. Use [performance methodology](performance.md)
for new comparisons and [compatibility](compatibility.md) for current support.
